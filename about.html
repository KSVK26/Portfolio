04 — Philosophy

The three principles I live by

Good planning and communication simplify execution. When everyone understands the objective and their role, complexity collapses into clear action.

People matter as much as the process. Rules and structures exist to serve humans — not the other way around. Fairness and context beat rigidity every time.

Ideas become valuable when built, evaluated and improved. Perfect is the enemy of done. Launch, learn, iterate — that's the only loop that compounds.
