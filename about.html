Who I am

I'm a designer, builder, and community organizer working at the intersection of craft, technology, and entrepreneurship. Most people call me Zoopi. I study how groups make decisions — and how to design better ways for them to do it.

I've studied design management at the University of the Arts London, spent a summer at Parsons in New York, worked within Philips Design, and co-founded a venture studio called Prismatic. My work has taken me across four countries and into rooms ranging from fashion runways to startup accelerators.
