Who I am

My interests have taken me from competitive gaming communities to tournament operations, event management, software projects, graphic design and digital campaigns. Each has taught me something different, and more importantly, given me a different way to approach problems.

Esports

The esports side

Competitive gaming was one of my earliest entry points into working with communities and organised competition. Over time, I moved beyond simply following the games. I became involved in tournament administration, competitive formats, match coordination, team communication and the operational work that keeps events running.

I've worked with tournament organisers, players, volunteers and creative teams. I've experienced the pressure of deadlines, unexpected problems and situations where someone has to step in and make a decision. It's where I learned that good execution is rarely accidental.

Technology

The technology side
