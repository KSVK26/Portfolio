Who I am

I'm K S Vasanth Karthik — known in the community as KSVK. I've spent the last five years running competitive Call of Duty Mobile tournaments at scale, from regional Asian leagues to major India-wide cups. My work sits at the intersection of operations, community management, and technical execution.

I've served as Regional Director for Zenith CoDM League (Asia), Event Manager for Esports XO's Champions League, Lead Admin for CODM India Clash and Ascend Cup, and moderator for Loco India Cup. Across 15+ tournaments, I've coordinated over 10,000 players and teams — handling bracket operations, rule enforcement, dispute resolution, and live match orchestration.

In 2026, I'm expanding into web development through a formal Web Programming course, building modern, performant applications and decoupled content systems. This portfolio is both a course project and a living archive of my esports operations career.

Philosophy

How I think about work

Operations

Clarity prevents chaos

In tournaments, a single ambiguous rule causes hours of disputes. I write rulebooks that leave no room for interpretation.

Community

Players remember how you treated them when things went wrong. Fairness > speed, always.

Iteration
