Ways of Working

How I work with people

The principles, tools, and rhythms that shape how I collaborate.

Principles

Clarity over cleverness

I'd rather write something a new teammate understands in five minutes than something impressive that needs decoding.

Decide, then commit

We debate ideas early and execute hard once a decision is made. No endless rework.

Ship small, learn fast

A working prototype beats a perfect plan. Iterate in public, learn from what breaks.
